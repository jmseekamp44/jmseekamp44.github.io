Where to Stay

Traverse City will be busy during the summer season. We recommend booking accommodations far in advance before hotels are fully booked.

We have a limited block of rooms reserved at the Park Place Hotel and at the Country Inn and Suites. A shuttle bus will be travelling between the venue and these two hotels before and after the wedding and reception.

The venue itself is a little outside of Traverse City (~10 min drive, 40 min walk to downtown TC). Rideshare platforms like Uber or Lyft are uncommon in the area and should not be relied upon for transportation to and from the venue.
